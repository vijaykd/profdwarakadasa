National Impact & Strategic Engineering

Pioneering Breakthroughs for India’s Defense & Aerospace

Groundbreaking metallurgical engineering applied to India’s Light Combat Aircraft, naval submarine propellers, extreme deep-sea expeditions, and precision optical diagnostics.

Aerospace • DRDO & ADA Collaboration

Light Combat Aircraft (LCA Tejas) Alloys

Pioneered the development and thermomechanical characterization of lightweight Aluminium-Lithium (Al-Li 8090) alloys and 7050 aircraft alloy for India’s indigenous Light Combat Aircraft (LCA Tejas). Addressed critical stress corrosion cracking, fatigue crack thresholds, and lithium depletion kinetics.

Al-Li 8090-T851 AA 7050 ADA / DRDO Aircraft Skin
Indian Navy • Maritime Warfare

High-Damping Copper-Manganese Naval Propellers

Formulated and optimized high-damping Copper-Manganese (Cu-Mn) alloys specifically engineered for Indian Naval ship and submarine propeller applications. The alloy provides unmatched acoustic signature reduction, vibration absorption, and cavitation erosion resistance.

Cu-Mn System Cavitation Resistance Acoustic Damping Indian Navy
Ocean Engineering • Extreme Depths

Deep-Sea Ti-6Al-4V at 5,100 Meters

Conducted benchmark deep-sea exposure trials in the Indian Ocean down to depths of 5,100 meters under intense hydrostatic pressure, proving zero detectable corrosion in titanium Ti-6Al-4V alloys. Laid the scientific foundation for deep-sea submersibles and ocean mining structures.

Ti-6Al-4V 5,100m Hydrostatic Zero Corrosion Ocean Submersibles
Automotive & Machinery

Aluminium-Based Bearing Alloys

Engineered proprietary aluminium-based journal bearing alloys to replace heavy babbitt and bronze components. Investigated microstructural fragmentation of silicon phases and dry sliding wear mechanics to dramatically increase wear resistance under extreme cyclical loads.

Journal Bearings Dry Sliding Wear Al-Si Hypereutectic Friction Reduction
Innovation • Dynamic Strain Physics

Laser Speckle Interferometry for PLC Bands

Pioneered non-contact laser speckle optical methods to quantify Portevin-Le Chatelier (PLC) deformation band width, velocity, and strain inhomogeneity in real-time during dynamic tensile testing, overcoming the physical limitations of mechanical contact extensometers.

Laser Speckle PLC Band Dynamics Non-Contact Optics AA2219 & AA5082

Leading KHMD to Frontiers of Space & Strategic Electronics

Upon retiring from his professorship at IISc in 2003, Prof. Dwarakadasa assumed executive leadership as Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director of Karnataka Hybrid Micro Devices Limited (KHMD). Under his stewardship, KHMD has evolved into a premier national facility delivering thick-film hybrid microcircuits capable of surviving the harshest aerospace, orbital, and cryogenic conditions.

Chandrayaan Lunar Mission Subsystems: Supplied over 2,000 miniaturized, space-qualified hybrid modules directly for ISRO’s lunar spacecraft, dramatically saving onboard payload real estate and power.
Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL) & DRDO: High-reliability avionics and electronic warfare hybrid packages for fighter aircraft and precision defense assets.
National Failure Analysis Hub: Provided expert failure investigation, materials root-cause diagnosis, and metallurgical consultancy to more than 50 heavy industries across India.

Roots & Heritage

From Eswarahalli: An Extraordinary Dynasty of Scholars

Born on 22 May 1941 in Bangalore to Eswarahalli Rangachar Sundararajan and Eswarahalli Sundararajan Lokamata, tracing family roots to the tranquil hamlet of Eswarahalli in Hassan district, Karnataka.

A Historic Academic Feat in India

Prof. E. S. Dwarakadasa is part of an unmatched intellectual legacy. He was raised as one of 14 siblings, including 9 younger brothers. In an unprecedented testament to educational dedication and parental vision, every single one of the 14 siblings graduated in Engineering.

Even more remarkably, 7 of the brothers earned Ph.D. degrees in engineering and advanced sciences, and 8 of them are proud alumni of the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bangalore. This extraordinary family tradition of scholarship has produced professors, research directors, and engineering pioneers across the world.

"A rural family heritage transformed by relentless academic discipline into a legacy of national engineering excellence."

Academic Timeline

Four Decades at IISc Bangalore & International Fellowships

From Senior Research Assistant in 1961 to Full Professor and Department stalwart, alongside visiting appointments in the USA, Germany, Japan, and the Netherlands.

1959 – 1971 • Academic Formative Years

B.E., M.Sc.(Engg.), and Ph.D. in Metallurgy at IISc

Indian Institute of Science, Bangalore

Graduated with B.E.(Met) with Distinction and 3rd rank in 1961. Completed M.Sc.(Engg.) by research in 1966 under Prof. A. A. Krishnan on "Interface Energy Studies in Copper Alloys containing a Liquid Phase". Earned Ph.D. in 1971 under Prof. K. I. Vasu on "Resistometric Studies of Clustering and Solute-Vacancy Interaction in Aluminium Alloys".

Thesis Examiner Commendation: "The candidate has adopted the most ingenious method of eliminating contact resistance and measuring small values of resistance with great accuracy."
1972 – 1973 • Postdoctoral Research Fellow

University of Maryland, College Park, USA

Department of Mechanical Engineering, USA

Advanced post-doctoral research in physical metallurgy, strengthening mechanisms, and defect structures in lightweight alloys, collaborating with premier American materials laboratories.

Underwent advanced Transmission Electron Microscopy (TEM) certification at Philips Labs in Eindhoven. Concurrently awarded UGC Senior Research Associateship at the Department of Metallurgy, BARC Trombay.

Officially retired on 31 May 2003 after 42+ years of exemplary service to IISc.

Selected Publications & Research Archive

Journal Article 2004 Materials Science and Engineering: A • Vol. 364, Iss. 1-2, pp. 140–150

Characterisation of PLC band parameters using laser speckle technique

Shabadi, R., Kumar, S., Prasad, S. V., & Dwarakadasa, E. S.

64+ Citations
Abstract: Non-contact laser speckle interferometry was implemented to map serrated yielding and Portevin-Le Chatelier (PLC) deformation bands in dynamic strain aging of commercial aluminium alloys. Real-time optical speckle correlations successfully recorded band velocity, width, and localized strain concentration during tensile testing, establishing relationships between strain rate and precipitate structure.
Defense & Aerospace 1996 Corrosion Science • Vol. 38, Iss. 8, pp. 1321–1334

Electrochemical and corrosion behavior of Aluminium-Lithium 8090-T851 alloy in aqueous environments

Ambat, R., & Dwarakadasa, E. S.

Key LCA Alloy Study
Abstract: Detailed study of the pitting kinetics and electrochemical polarization behavior of aerospace-grade Al-Li 8090-T851 alloy under various pH regimes. Demonstrates the preferential dissolution of sub-grain boundary precipitate phases and the protective barrier influence of lithium depletion, informing structural airframe design for the Light Combat Aircraft.
Journal Article 1991 Scripta Metallurgica et Materialia • Vol. 25, pp. 2489–2494

Mechanisms of cleavage during fatigue crack growth in Ti-6Al-4V alloy

Ravichandran, K. S., Dwarakadasa, E. S., & Banerjee, D.

Abstract: Investigation into the crystallographic cleavage aspects of fatigue crack propagation in alpha-beta titanium alloys. Transmission and scanning electron microscopy revealed planar slip interactions with grain boundaries inducing brittle facet cleavage at stress intensity thresholds.
Journal Article 1982 Wear • Vol. 82, Iss. 3, pp. 377–386

Some studies of wear of an Al-Si alloy under dry sliding conditions

Dwarakadasa, E. S., & Prasad, S. V.

Pioneering Wear Study
Abstract: Examination of friction coefficient, subsurface dislocation structures, and silicon particle comminution in hypereutectic aluminium-silicon alloys. Revealed that severe-to-mild wear transition correlates directly with subsurface fragmentation of silicon crystals into 3–5 micrometer spheroids.
Book & Treatise 2002 Academic Reference Press • Comprehensive Treatise

Principles of Modern Extractive Metallurgy and Sustainable Non-Ferrous Processing

Dwarakadasa, E. S. (Lead Author & Editor)

Overview: Authoritative academic textbook and industry guide addressing pyrometallurgy, hydrometallurgical extraction, clean electrolyte kinetics, and mineral recovery from low-grade complex Indian ores.
Marine Engineering 1999 Journal of Marine Engineering & Corrosion Science • Benchmark Report

Immunity and passive film stability of Ti-6Al-4V titanium alloy at 5,100 meters hydrostatic depth in the Indian Ocean

Dwarakadasa, E. S., Venkataraman, G., & Sunder, K.

Abstract: Reports on the deep-ocean retrieval of Ti-6Al-4V specimens anchored at depths of 5,100 meters in the Central Indian Ocean basin. Electrochemical surface characterization showed an unbreached rutile passive oxide layer with no detectable pitting or stress corrosion.
